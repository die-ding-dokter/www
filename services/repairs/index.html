Repairs FAQ

Useful things to know.

How much will a repair cost?

Pricing depends on the specific fault, device and required parts. Ask for an assessment and quotation rather than relying on a generic price.

Should I back up my files?

If possible, back up important data before any troubleshooting or repair. Let the team know if the device contains essential information.

Do you offer a repair warranty?

Please confirm the applicable repair and parts warranty in writing with the team before authorising any work.

Can I drop off my device?

Contact us first to confirm the correct service location, opening hours and handover arrangements.
