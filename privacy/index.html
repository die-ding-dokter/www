When you use WhatsApp, Facebook, an external shopping storefront or an email application, those services may process personal information according to their own privacy policies. Review their notices before submitting sensitive information.

Our hosting provider may process routine technical connection data such as IP addresses and access logs to deliver and secure the website. This site does not include third-party advertising or analytics scripts by default.

Your choices

For questions about messages you have sent or to make a privacy request, contact sales@diedingdokter.co.za. Avoid including passwords or other sensitive credentials in enquiries.
